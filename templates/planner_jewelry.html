Jewellery money rules

Making charges run 10-25% of metal value and are the easiest place to overshoot - always ask for them in writing. Gold rates move daily, so we keep a 3-5% buffer. Lab-grown diamonds give the same look for 40-60% less.

Never skip

BIS hallmark on gold, IGI or AGS certification on diamonds, and a written exchange/buy-back policy. We include them as line items.
